SAMPLE CONTRACT AGREEMENT

This Agreement is made between ABC Company ("Company") and John Doe ("Employee").

TERMS AND CONDITIONS:

1. Employment Period: The employee will work for a period of 2 years starting January 1, 2024.

2. Salary: The monthly salary will be $5,000 paid on the last day of each month.

3. Benefits: The employee is entitled to:
   - Health insurance coverage
   - 15 days of paid vacation annually
   - Sick leave as per company policy

4. Termination: Either party may terminate this contract with 30 days written notice.

5. Confidentiality: The employee agrees to maintain confidentiality of all company information.

6. Governing Law: This contract is governed by the laws of California.

Signed on December 15, 2023.
