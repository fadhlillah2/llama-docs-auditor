This is a sample contract for testing purposes with Groq integration.

PARTIES
This agreement is made between John Doe (hereinafter "Client") and ABC Legal Services (hereinafter "Provider").

SERVICES
The Provider agrees to provide legal consulting services to the Client for a period of 12 months.

PAYMENT TERMS
The Client agrees to pay the Provider $5,000 per month for the services rendered. Payments are due within 30 days of invoice.

LIABILITY
The Provider's liability under this agreement shall not exceed $50,000 in total.

TERMINATION
Either party may terminate this agreement with 30 days written notice.

GOVERNING LAW
This agreement shall be governed by the laws of the State of California.

This contract is effective as of January 1, 2024.
